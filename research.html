Research

Research

EPIC Lab aims to understand and engineer electrochemical interfaces by bridging chemistry, mechanics, and processing,
advancing next-generation energy storage technologies.

Electrochemical Interface Engineering

We focus on controlling interfacial phenomena in batteries through integrated approaches spanning electrolyte chemistry, system mechanics, and surface engineering. Electrochemical interfaces govern the performance, safety, and lifetime of energy storage systems, and our goal is to understand and control interphase formation and evolution, providing a unified framework for engineering electrochemical interfaces across energy storage technologies.

01 · (Electro)chemical control

Electrolyte & Interface Design

We design electrolytes and investigate solvation structures to control interphase formation (SEI/CEI) in lithium and sodium-based batteries.

ZOOM +
02 · Mechanical control

Pressure & System-Level Engineering

We study the role of pressure and cell-level conditions in governing interfacial contact, safety, and electrochemical performance.

ZOOM +
03 · Process control

Vapor-Phase Surface Engineering

We utilize vapor-phase techniques such as atomic layer deposition (ALD) to precisely engineer artificial interphases and stabilize electrochemical interfaces.
